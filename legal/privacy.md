# Salony Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 20, 2026_

Salony ("we", "us", "our") is a salon and barbershop booking platform. This policy
describes what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it
with, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it short. If anything is
unclear, email **support@salony.app** and we'll fix the wording.

## 1. Who we are

Salony is operated by the team behind salony.app, based in Egypt. We are the
data controller for personal data submitted through our mobile and web apps.

## 2. The data we collect

| Category | What | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email, phone, password (hashed) | Sign-in, account recovery, booking confirmations |
| Profile | Optional avatar, preferences | Personalised recommendations |
| Bookings | Salons, services, dates, prices, tips | The booking flow itself; salon-side history |
| Location | GPS coordinates (foreground only) | Sorting "salons near you" by distance |
| Photos | Selfies you upload to AI Hairstyle, review photos | Generating the AI preview; attaching photos to your review |
| Payments | Card last-4, transaction status | Receipts and refunds. We never see full card numbers — they go straight to the payment processor (Stripe / Paymob). |
| Device | App version, OS, crash logs | Bug fixing |
| Usage | Screens viewed, taps, search queries | Understanding which features matter |

We do **not** collect: precise advertising identifiers, social-graph data,
contacts from your phone, or anything from background location.

## 3. How we use it

- Provide the service you signed up for (bookings, messaging a salon, paying).
- Personalise recommendations (nearby salons, suggested services).
- Generate AI hairstyle previews. Selfies you upload are sent to our chosen
  image-generation provider (Replicate or OpenAI), processed to produce a
  preview, and not stored by Salony after the preview is returned. The
  provider's own retention policy applies — see the linked policies below.
- Send transactional emails/SMS (booking confirmations, password reset).
- Detect fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers.

## 4. Who we share it with (sub-processors)

| Sub-processor | What they see | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud hosting (Render / Fly / DigitalOcean) | All app data, encrypted at rest | Running the backend |
| Stripe / Paymob | Name, email, payment amount | Processing payments |
| Anthropic / OpenAI | Salon name & marketing brief (no customer data) | Marketing copy generation (barbers only) |
| Replicate / OpenAI | Your selfie + style prompt | AI hairstyle preview generation |
| Expo / EAS | Crash logs, app version | Mobile build & OTA delivery |

Each sub-processor has its own privacy policy. If you want a current list with
links, email us.

## 5. Where we store data

Primary storage is in EU/EEA data centres. Some processing (AI image
generation, payment processing) happens in the US under standard contractual
clauses. You can request deletion at any time.

## 6. Your rights

You can:

- View, edit or delete your profile from **Profile → Edit profile**.
- Export all your bookings as CSV by emailing **support@salony.app**.
- Delete your account from **Profile → Delete account** (at the bottom of the
  Profile screen), or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent and removes your
  bookings, points, and saved data; we cannot recover it afterwards.
- Withdraw location and camera permissions at any time from your phone's
  settings — the app will keep working with reduced personalisation.

Under GDPR / Egyptian Data Protection Law, you also have the right to lodge a
complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 7. Children

Salony is for users aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from
under-13s. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll
remove it.

## 8. Cookies and tracking

The mobile app does not use cookies. The web booking page (salony.app/{handle})
uses a single first-party session cookie for sign-in. No third-party trackers,
no advertising cookies.

## 9. Retention

- Account data: kept while your account is open, deleted within 30 days of
  account deletion.
- Booking history: kept for 7 years to meet tax reporting requirements.
- Photos: review photos kept while the review is live; AI selfies not stored
  on our servers.
- Logs: 30 days for application logs, 90 days for security logs.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we'll notify you in-app and via email at least
14 days before the change takes effect.
